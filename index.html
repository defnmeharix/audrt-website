What AUDRT does

Everything you need to clean up vocals in one lightweight VST3.

🎙

Live Noise Capture

Record a few seconds of ambient silence. AUDRT builds a noise profile and subtracts it from your audio using spectral subtraction.

🎶

File-Based Profile

Already have a silence recording? Load it as WAV, AIFF or FLAC and AUDRT will use that as the noise floor reference.

🎻

Noise Mix Knob

Dial in exactly how much noise to cut — 10% for gentle softening up to 100% aggressive removal. Start around 50%.

🎤

Pitch Correction

Auto-tune vocals to the nearest note in any key and scale — Major, Minor, Pentatonic, Blues, or Chromatic.

🌊

Live Waveform

Watch your audio come in live as a waveform. Confirm the plugin is receiving signal and monitor levels at a glance.

📏

Pitch Meter

A real-time cents display shows how far your pitch is from the target. Green = in tune, red = off pitch.

💾

Preset Save / Load

Save your settings as XML presets and reload them anytime. Perfect for switching between mics or rooms.

⚡

Latency Control

Four latency modes let you trade CPU usage for response time — Ultra Low for live performance, High for resource-limited sessions.

How to install

Works with Ableton Live 12 and any VST3-compatible DAW on macOS.

Download the zip

Click Download above or visit GitHub Releases to get AUDRT.vst3.zip

Unzip it

Double-click the zip. You'll see a folder called AUDRT.vst3 — this is the plugin bundle.

Move to VST3 folder

Move the entire folder here:~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

Rescan in Ableton

Preferences → Plug-ins → click Rescan. AUDRT appears in your VST3 library.

Load & go

Drag AUDRT onto a track. Click Live → Capture Noise and you're ready.
